Common questions

Is Para really free?
Yes. The chat assistant, document scanner, and browser extension are all free to use. We built Para because quality legal information shouldn't depend on being able to afford a lawyer.
Is this legal advice?
No. Para provides general legal information and education — it helps you understand your situation, your rights, and your options. It is not a substitute for a licensed attorney, and no attorney-client relationship is created. For advice on your specific case, consult a lawyer (Para can point you to free legal aid).
Where do Para's answers come from?
Para grounds its answers in real, public legal data — including CourtListener, the Free Law Project's database of over 9 million court decisions, and official government sources. When Para cites a case, it links to the actual decision so you can read it yourself.
Is my information private?
Your conversations are processed only to generate your answer and are never sold. Still, avoid pasting sensitive identifiers (Social Security numbers, account numbers) — Para doesn't need them to help you.
What languages does Para support?
English, Spanish, and Chinese today — with more languages coming. Use the buttons in the top bar to switch at any time.
How do I find a real lawyer if I need one?
Start with LawHelp.org for free legal aid in your area, or your state bar association's lawyer-referral service. If your issue involves work, housing, or debt, agencies like the EEOC, HUD, and CFPB take complaints directly — and Para can tell you which one fits your situation.

Install the Extension

The Para browser extension automatically detects Terms of Service pages and scans them for red flags — without you having to do anything.

How to install
1. Click Download Extension below
2. Unzip the downloaded file
3. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
4. Turn on Developer Mode (top right)
5. Click Load unpacked → select the unzipped folder
6. The 🛡️ icon will appear in your toolbar
